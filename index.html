Personal project

SharedLens

Serverless photo and video gallery

May 2025 – Present · AWS S3, Cognito, Firebase

Used at five weddings with more than 100 guests. I built a fully client-side gallery where guests on iOS and Android can browse, upload, see photo credits, and remove their own media without an account.

Visit SharedLensView sourceTry the thumbnail loader
Prioritized loading
I used IntersectionObserver and a distance heuristic to reprioritize thumbnails as visitors scroll. The loader caps concurrent requests at four. Compressed WebP thumbnails for photos and videos reduce storage and bandwidth.
Uploads and ownership
Amazon Cognito Identity Pools provide temporary credentials. The gallery tracks uploader ownership in localStorage and shows delete controls for media that browser uploaded. Large files use multipart S3 uploads with 5 MB chunks and live progress.
Custom gallery URLs
I used Firebase Hosting, Authentication, and a database to let organizers create custom paths on sharedlens.ca.
